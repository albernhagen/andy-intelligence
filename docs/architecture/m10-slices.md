# M10: Paid models + spend caps — slices

Slice table for [milestones.md §M10](milestones.md#m10-paid-models--spend-caps). Epic: AI-226: M10: Paid models + spend caps. Each ticket holds its acceptance criteria, out of scope and full `touches` (files it owns). **Merge** says who merges under ADR-0120: the coordinator, once checks and both reviews are clean, or Andy, for one of his four kinds: `judges` (changes `.github/**` or `platform/actions/**`), `hands` (needs his key, account, spend or `terraform apply`).

**Already built, not sliced:** `Provider`, `Model`, `Tier` schemas with cap fields (contracts, unused); `RunRequest.billingType`; subscription cost estimates (`domain/cost.ts`); quota capture, reserves and dispatch caps (ADR-0079, ADR-0100).

**Split with M9: Add Jules + model routing:** M9 owns provider and routing entities (`RoutingConfig`, `Model`, `Tier`, `Route`, `tiers.ts`, the resolver). M10 owns spend, caps and budgets. It reads M9's entities without editing them, except for one line each in the hot spots below.

**Design in one paragraph** (recorded by M10.1 as ADR-0129, proposed): every paid agent task or call **reserves** its worst-case cost in a spend ledger before it starts, then **settles** to the actual cost, or keeps the full reservation when usage is unknown. A reservation that would pass a per-task, project or global daily or monthly cap is refused, and dispatch idles with `spend_cap`. Anything unknown (ledger unreadable, model without a price) **fails closed**: no paid call. A second cap layer sits at the provider: the OpenRouter key's credit limit and the Anthropic workspace spend limit, at or below our monthly cap. Subscription routes are unchanged.
