# Ticket panel

A ticket opens over the board led by the decision (AI-336). Under the status line, **Next** shows the stage, the next move as the panel's one primary button, then whether the Definition of Ready is met, the open PR and any open questions. **Move…** opens the other allowed moves, Up next and Force move. The rail beside the description is **Details** (type, size, epic, milestone, blocked by), then **How it runs**, collapsed with its values in one line (worker, runs on, tier, model, override count): open it for the worker and executor switches, overrides and routing. Cancel and archive sit last. An epic leads with its outcome, its tickets with progress, its milestone and Plan the night, then its description and lists. Every other button on the panel is outline.

**How to use:** click a card on the board, or open `/p/<project>/t/<KEY>`; MCP `move_ticket`, `set_worker`, `set_executor` and `edit_ticket` do the same
