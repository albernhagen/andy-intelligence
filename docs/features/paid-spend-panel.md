# Paid spend panel

Dashboards shows **Paid spend**: today and month to date for each project and for all projects, against the caps in force, with the share used (highlighted at 80% and at the cap). Spend the ledger can't read shows as unknown, never $0. Pay-per-use models only; subscription usage stays in the quota banner (AI-257: M10.9: Spend panel and cap alerts). Spend by provider, and settled vs reserved, aren't shown yet: the ledger keeps totals per project and for all projects only.

**How to use:** open Dashboards for one project or for all projects; set caps in [project settings](spend-caps.md).
