# Epic state follows its tickets

An epic's stage follows its tickets (AI-404, ADR-0164); nobody moves it. No tickets, or all in Backlog: **Not started**. One past Backlog: **In progress**. All Done (Cancelled ones don't count): **Done**, with a history entry by the system ("follows its tickets: N of M done"). A new or reopened ticket under a Done epic makes it In progress again. A child epic counts by its own state, so nesting rolls up. With **Needs your check** on (off by default), an epic whose tickets are all Done shows **Waiting for your check** until Andy closes it. The board, roadmap, milestone page, MCP `status` and `milestone_map` all read this one state. Epics are left out of board lanes and per-stage counts (web, MCP `board` and `status`, dashboards).

**How to use:** nothing to do: the epic panel says "Its state follows its tickets". Turn on **Needs your check** in the epic panel's Details (or MCP `edit_ticket` with `needsCheck: true`); close a waiting epic with **Close epic** (or MCP `move_ticket` to `done`). Any other move of an epic is refused. Existing epics: run the fix-up in [`runbooks/epic-state-fixup.md`](../runbooks/epic-state-fixup.md) once.
