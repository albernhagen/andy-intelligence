# Tickets

Projects; tickets move Backlog → … → Done/Cancelled by drag or buttons (Andy can force any move; while dragged, a copy of the card follows the pointer, its slot stays as a dashed placeholder and the lane under it lights up, AI-396); a title suffices; type sets the Definition of Ready (spike: a findings doc), size the tier; tickets open over the board: status line, markdown, in-place edits, one timeline; archive hides finished ones. Backlog zones (drag or button): Up next (ordered, refined), Ideas (parked, no agent spend); a card moved back from a later stage lands in Ideas wherever it is dropped (AI-395), so putting it in Up next is a second, deliberate step. Cards: size, risk, confidence (set by refine, on the ticket page or with MCP `edit_ticket`, which also sets Needs-you); Ready sorts by them (`?sort=score`, rank unchanged) with why. Epics: an outcome line (ideate and refine propose it; refine never replaces one) and a "Done when" list, group, filter, child tickets, cost, cascading overrides, refine into stories with blockers. Cross-project blockers
