# Night morning report

When a night launch ends, one short comment on the epic: why it ended, child tickets merged, stopped and not started, spend against the budget, the morning PR, and per ticket its PR, checks, review findings, night assumptions and cost. Stopped tickets say why; night assumptions are quoted as agent text. The epic is flagged Needs-you, so the inbox and email show it. Posted once per launch, from stored facts only (AI-125, ADR-0049).

**How to use:** nothing to set: it follows a night launch. In the morning open the epic from the inbox; steps in [runbook overnight-runs.md](../runbooks/overnight-runs.md) §4.
