# 2026-11-21 — AI-257: M10.9: Spend panel and cap alerts

Read-only panel and cap notices for M10: Paid models + spend caps. Nothing spends money.

- **Panel** (`apps/web/src/components/spend/SpendPanel.tsx`, one mount line on Dashboards, after DORA): per project and for all projects, today and this month against the caps in force, from AI-249's `GET /api/projects/{projectId}/spend`. The all-projects view reads at most 20 projects (ADR-0028). Unknown spend shows as "unknown". No new route.
- **Notices** (`packages/domain/src/spend-notices.ts`, `spendNotices`): a scope at 80% of a daily or monthly cap (project or global), and a scope stopped at a cap naming up to 5 waiting ticket keys. Deduplicated by cap, scope and UTC window (global caps once, whichever project reaches them); at most 10 a UTC day; old windows pruned from the kept state. Numbers, keys and fixed phrases only: anything that isn't a ticket key is dropped, so no agent text reaches a notice. A zero cap (paid off) and unknown spend never warn.
- **By provider** (after AI-280: M10: Spend ledger keeps totals per provider): `GET /api/projects/{projectId}/spend/providers` (new module `spend-providers.ts`, project in the path so `project-access.ts` checks it) returns the project's and the global per-provider totals, null when unreadable. The panel lists providers with spend, today and this month.
- **Sending** (after AI-248: M10.7: Spend gate at dispatch): the gate calls an optional alert sink after a reservation (totals with it counted) and on a refusal at a cap. `SpendAlerts` runs `spendNotices` inside one `SpendNoticeLedger` update (new port, fake, contract suite; Firestore `config/spendNotices`, transactional, so two instances can't send the same notice) and sends through the Needs-you email `Notifier`; with email off, a WARNING log line. It never throws, so a notice can't block dispatch.
- **Not built:** settled vs reserved in the panel (no ledger read for it; not asked by the reviewer).
- Tests: 17 for the notices and their wiring through the gate, 11 for the panel, 3 for the provider route, the notice ledger's contract suite on the fake and the emulator.
